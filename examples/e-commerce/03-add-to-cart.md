# Add Product to Shopping Cart

On the product details page:

Look for the "Add to Cart" or "Add to Bag" button

Click the "Add to Cart" button

Wait for a confirmation that the product was added:
- This might be a popup message
- Or the cart icon might update with a count
- Or you might be redirected to the cart page

## Expected Outcome

Product is successfully added to the shopping cart with confirmation shown.
