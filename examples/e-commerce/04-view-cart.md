# View Shopping Cart

Navigate to the shopping cart by:
- Clicking on the cart icon in the header/navigation
- Or if already on the cart page after adding, verify you're there

Verify the cart contents:
- The product we added should be listed
- The product name should match what we selected
- The price should be displayed
- Quantity should be shown (likely 1)
- A subtotal or total should be visible

Verify there's a "Checkout", "Proceed to Checkout", or similar button

## Expected Outcome

Shopping cart displays the added product with correct details and a checkout option.
