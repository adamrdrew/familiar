# Verify Checkout Form

Verify that we've reached the checkout form with:
- Fields for shipping or billing address
- Fields for payment information
- A summary of the order (product, price, total)
- A final "Place Order" or "Complete Purchase" button

Do NOT click the final purchase button - we're just verifying the flow.

Verify the order summary shows:
- The product we added
- Correct quantity
- A reasonable total price

## Expected Outcome

Complete checkout form is displayed with order summary.
All steps from homepage to checkout were successful.

**This test intentionally stops here to avoid creating real orders.**
