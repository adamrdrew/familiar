# Select a Product

From the search results, identify a product that:
- Matches the search term "${PRODUCT_SEARCH}"
- Has a price displayed
- Appears to be in stock (no "Out of Stock" indicator)

Click on the product to view its details page

Wait for the product details page to load

Verify that the product details page shows:
- Product name and image
- Price information
- "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" button
- Product description or specifications

## Expected Outcome

Product details page loads with all essential information displayed.
